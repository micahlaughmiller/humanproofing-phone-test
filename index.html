REAL FACE MATCH · EARLY TEST

Try the face check

First, use the rear camera to show the photo on your ID. Then look straight at the front camera.

No sign-in pass is issued by this test.

Before you start

  1. Use good light. Keep glare off the ID.
  2. Point the camera at the ID photo. Keep other faces out of view. You can cover the name and numbers.
  3. Hold the phone still and look straight ahead. This version uses FacePlugin’s photo check instead of head turns.

Having trouble? Try “Test camera only” first. It needs no ID.

What this test can tell us

It compares the faces using FacePlugin’s public code. Its live-face check passed still photos in our test. It cannot yet prove that a real person is there. It does not check if the ID is real and is not a 3D scan.

No account is approved. No human pass is saved.

How your data stays here

The page downloads its face tools from this site before opening the camera. It does not send your pictures or face data to FacePlugin or another server. Images and face data are used in memory only. The page stops the camera, clears its image buffers, and drops face data at the end or when you leave it.

The browser controls memory, so this is not a promise of secure memory erasure. The site host still sees the normal page request. We do not use photo uploads, storage, or tracking code.
